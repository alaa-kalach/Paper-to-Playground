Input: source_url, focus (the concept + required learning outcomes), audience.

- Explain only the concept in focus. Every learning outcome in focus must be covered by a control, an output, or an exploration.
- Write for the audience. Short, clear sentences. Define every symbol.
- All equations and symbols in LaTeX.
- paper.section and paper.equation: fill only if named in focus or you are certain; otherwise null.
